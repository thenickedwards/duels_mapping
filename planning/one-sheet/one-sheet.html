What is Duels Mapping?

Duels Mapping measures how soccer players win the ball. Every player in MLS* is scored and ranked using stats related to contesting possession of the ball.
* More leagues coming soon!
duels-mapping.vercel.app

The Schmetzer Score

Five ball-winning stats are weighted and combined into one composite metric. (Named for Sounders coach Brian Schmetzer, whose favorite stat to open post-game pressers is duels won.)

+1.5Tackle won
+1Aerial duel won
+0.9Interception
+0.25Recovery
−0.85Aerial duel lost
Disagree with the weights? Hit Tuning to customize your own, rankings update instantly.

Why is Duels Mapping useful?

Credit for the dirty workGoals get headlines, but someone has to win the tackles and headers that defend their net and launch attacks.
Bang for your buckMLSPA salary data alongside each score shows club spending per ball won.
Historical data since 2018Ranked and searchable player data for 9 seasons, 30 clubs, 2,372 players, and 6,752 unique player‑seasons.

Ways to use Duels Mapping

Tactics & strategy
  • Compare two players to prep for 1v1 matchups in-game
  • Exploit opponent weaknesses on set pieces and in the run of play
  • Pinpoint players who break up attacks and trigger counters
The business of soccer
  • Sort by cost per ball won to uncover undervalued players
  • Identify breakout youngsters and veterans who have lost a step
  • Filter by position, age and minutes to build a scouting shortlist
Stories & talking points
  • Pull a stat for a match preview: who wins the midfield battle?
  • Backed by the numbers: is your center back good in the air?
  • Follow players’ rise or decline across seasons
