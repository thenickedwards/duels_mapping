What is Duels Mapping?

Duels Mapping measures how soccer players win the ball. Every MLS player is scored and ranked using stats related to contesting possession of the ball.
duels-mapping.vercel.app

The Schmetzer Score

Five ball-winning stats are weighted and combined into one composite metric. (Named for Sounders coach Brian Schmetzer and his favorite stat: duels won.)

+1.5Tackle won
+1Aerial duel won
+0.9Interception
+0.25Recovery
−0.85Aerial duel lost
Disagree with the weights? Hit Tuning to customize your own, rankings update instantly.

Why is Duels Mapping useful?

Credit for the dirty workGoals get headlines, but someone has to win the tackles and duels that keep the ball out of their own net and set up scorers to score.
Bang for your buckMLSPA salary data alongside each score, showing club spend per ball won.
Nine seasons deepHistorical player data tracked back to 2018, ranked and searchable. 30 clubs, 2,372 players, and 6,752 unique player‑seasons.

Ways to use Duels Mapping

Tactics & strategy
  • Compare two players before a key 1v1 matchup
  • Exploit opponent weaknesses on set pieces and in the run of play
  • Find players who win the ball back to start counter-attacks
The business of soccer
  • Sort by cost per ball won to spot undervalued players
  • Identify breakout youngsters and veterans who have lost a step
  • Filter by position, age and minutes to build a scouting shortlist
Stories & talking points
  • Pull a stat for a match preview: who wins the midfield battle?
  • Settle the debate: is your center back actually good in the air?
  • Follow a player’s rise or decline across seasons
